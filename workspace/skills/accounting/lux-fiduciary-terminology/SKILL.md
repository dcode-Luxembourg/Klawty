# Luxembourg Fiduciary Terminology

Provides the correct terminology for Luxembourg accounting and tax concepts across the three administrative languages (French, German, English). Ensures agent communications use the precise terms expected by the AED, ACD, CSSF, and clients.

## Musts

- Always use the official French term when referencing a legal concept in formal documents — Luxembourg law is primarily in French.
- Never translate legal entity types loosely: SARL is not "LLC," SA is not "Inc." — use the Luxembourg designations.
- Use the correct administration names: AED (Administration de l'Enregistrement et des Domaines) for VAT, ACD (Administration des Contributions Directes) for income tax.
- Never abbreviate without defining: first use should be the full term followed by the abbreviation in parentheses.

## Guidelines

- Key entity types: SARL (Societe a Responsabilite Limitee), SA (Societe Anonyme), SCS (Societe en Commandite Simple), SCSp (Societe en Commandite Speciale), SCA (Societe en Commandite par Actions).
- Tax terms: IRC (Impot sur le Revenu des Collectivites = corporate income tax), ICC (Impot Commercial Communal = municipal business tax), IF (Impot sur la Fortune = net wealth tax), RTS (Retenue d'Impot sur les Traitements et Salaires = wage withholding tax).
- VAT terms: TVA (Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutee), autoliquidation (reverse charge), listing intracommunautaire (EC Sales List), declaration TVA (VAT return).
- Filing platforms: eCDF (electronic filing for annual accounts), MyGuichet (citizen/business portal), eTVA (VAT return filing).
- Accounting terms: bilan (balance sheet), compte de profits et pertes (P&L), immobilisations (fixed assets), creances (receivables), dettes (payables), charges (expenses), produits (revenues), amortissements (depreciation).

## Common Actions

### Terminology lookup

When drafting a communication or document, reference this skill to use the correct term in the appropriate language. Formal documents to administrations use French. Client communications use the client's preferred language with French legal terms where precision matters.

### Entity type reference

When onboarding a client or preparing filings, verify the entity type and its implications: minimum capital (SARL: 12,000 EUR, SA: 30,000 EUR), governance requirements, and applicable tax regimes.

### Administration routing

When a filing or inquiry is needed, reference the correct administration: AED for VAT and registration duties, ACD for income tax and net wealth tax, CCSS (Centre Commun de la Securite Sociale) for social security, RCSL for commercial register filings.
